# Exploratory Data Analysis

## 0. Duplicated Data
- **Count:**
    - How many rows are duplicated in the dataset
## 1. Descriptive Statistics:
- **Frequency:**
    - Count of occurrences for categorical variables.
- **Location Measures:**
    - Mean: Average value.
    - Median: Middle value.
    - Mode: Most frequent value.
- **Spread Measures:**
    - Variance: Measure of data dispersion.
    - Standard Deviation: Measure of data variability.
    - Range: Difference between the maximum and minimum values.
    - Interquartile Range (IQR): Range between the first and third quartiles.
- **Percentiles:**
    - Provide insight into data distribution at various levels.
- **Skewness:**
    - Measure of asymmetry in the distribution.
- **Kurtosis:**
    - Measure of the "tailedness" of the distribution.
- **Missing Ratio:**
    - Percentage of missing values for each attribute.

## 2. Data Distribution Visualization:
- **Histograms:**
    - Visual representation of frequency distribution.
- **Box Plots:**
    - Display of median, quartiles, and outliers.
- **Density Plots:**
    - Smoothed representation of the distribution.
- **QQ Plots:**
    - Comparison between sample quantiles and theoretical quantiles.

## 3. Correlation Analysis:
- **Correlation Coefficients:**
    - Pearson, Spearman, or Kendall correlation coefficients.
- **Correlation Matrix:**
    - Visualization of correlation between attributes.
- **Heatmaps:**
    - Visual representation of correlation matrix.

## 4. Outlier Detection:
- **Z-Score:**
    - Measure of how many standard deviations an observation is from the mean.
- **IQR Method:**
    - Detect outliers based on quartile ranges.
- **Box Plots:**
    - Identify observations lying outside the whiskers.

## 5. Data Quality Assessment:
- **Unique Values:**
    - Count of unique values for each attribute.
- **Data Consistency Checks:**
    - Ensuring data adheres to defined standards.
- **Data Completeness:**
    - Assessing the percentage of missing values.
- **Data Accuracy:**
    - Comparing against known benchmarks or external sources.
- **Data Relevance:**
    - Evaluating the importance of attributes for analysis.

## 6. Time-Series Analysis (if applicable; not relevant here):
- **Trend Analysis:**
    - Identifying long-term patterns.
- **Seasonality Detection:**
    - Recognizing repeating patterns over time.
- **Lag Analysis:**
    - Assessing correlation between observations at different time intervals.

## 7. Text Analysis (if applicable; not relevant here):
- **Word Frequency:**
    - Count of words in text data.
- **TF-IDF (Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency):**
    - Measure of importance of a word in a document relative to a corpus.
- **Sentiment Analysis:**
    - Determining the sentiment conveyed in text data.
